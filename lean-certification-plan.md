# Lean certification plan

**Status.** Proposed work. No Lean theorem from this manuscript has been compiled or replayed. The first deliverable is a kernel-checked proof that the vacuum weights have no common limit under the stated finite-sector kernel. The sharper even limit and the embedding of the pulse are separate deliverables.

## Mathematical interface

Take complex constants \(A,C\) with \(A\ne0\), \(C\ne0\). For sector size \(N\ge1\) the input optical labels have normalization \(X=N^{-1/2}\sum x_i\). Define the vacuum amplitude by the coefficient of \(x_1\cdots x_N\) in \(C\exp(AX^2/2)\). One derivative in each independent label gives the same coefficient. Phases of modulus one can be omitted. Define the nonnegative weight by \(\lVert K_N\rVert^{2/N}\) using a **real** exponent after casting \(N\) to \(\mathbb R\). Any arbitrary value at \(N=0\) must be shown irrelevant to the sequence limit.

## Work packages and acceptance tests

1. **Finite coefficient extraction.** Work in complex multivariate polynomials on \(\mathrm{Fin}\,N\). Use the truncation of the exponential through degree \(N\); degrees above \(N\) cannot contribute to the square-free monomial. Odd total degree gives zero. For \(N=2m>0\), prove the coefficient of the product of the labels in the \((2m)\)-th power of their sum is \((2m)!\), and derive \(K_{2m}=C(2m)!(A/(4m))^m/m!\). Handle the collective square without adjoining square roots by writing \(X^2=(\sum_i x_i)^2/N\). Verify \(N=1,2,4\) to catch indexing and normalization slips. A theorem starting from the closed amplitude alone must be labeled conditional on coefficient extraction.
2. **Main obstruction with elementary bounds.** For \(m\ge1\), \((2m)!/m!=\prod_{j=1}^m(m+j)\ge m^m\). The finite expression gives \(W_{2m}\ge\lVert C\rVert^{1/m}\lVert A\rVert/4\), whose lower bound tends to \(\lVert A\rVert/4>0\). The odd subsequence is identically zero. Compose a hypothetical convergence theorem with both cofinal subsequences, use uniqueness of limits, and derive a contradiction. For the literal tower product prove that the \(n=1\) zero forces the modulus to vanish at every positive cutoff with positive root exponent. This milestone alone covers the main obstruction and the numerical-sequence comparison.
3. **Sharp even limit.** Use pinned Mathlib's \(\texttt{Stirling.factorial_isEquivalent_stirling}\) twice, at \(m\) and \(2m\), to show the normalized factorial ratio tends to \(\sqrt2\). Show its positive \(1/m\)-th power and \(\lVert C\rVert^{1/m}\) tend to one. Deduce \(W_{2m}\to\lVert A\rVert/e\), with \(\exp(1)\) in Lean. Cover \(A=0\) separately as an exact zero check. State \(m\to\infty\) at fixed coefficients. [Pinned Stirling lemma](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/db584cd6d46c92f209a44c0f1c829460d327499d/Mathlib/Analysis/SpecialFunctions/Stirling.lean)
4. **Optional pulse and supporting endpoint results.** First certify the two exact normal-mode identities using positive square roots and rational \(d=21/221\). A distinct bridge must derive the collective Hessian from the pulse or explicitly assume its expansion \(A(h)=-i\alpha h+O(h^2)\), \(\alpha>0\). Only the former verifies the dynamical premise. An optional final extension can formalize the bright correction series and the dark coefficient asymptotic, including the strict gap in the moduli of its two singularities. A proof using the termwise derivative at fixed replica count would not establish the fixed-\(h\) limit in the paper.

## Reproducibility and proof boundary

Use Lean 4.33.0 and Mathlib commit \(\texttt{db584cd6d46c92f209a44c0f1c829460d327499d}\), the pinned pair used in the [earlier Comment](https://github.com/Quantyra/cwl-common-drive-comment/tree/d56cd79ddd44d37a6fe5baf7faec0a13a06dfaa7/lean). Its permanent expansion concerns passive transitions; it does not supply the vacuum pair contractions. Keep theorem statements, formal dependencies and postulates visible. Require a clean build with warnings treated as errors, no placeholders or added axioms, an axiom report on exported theorems, and kernel replay of the compiled modules against the pinned imports. Archive the Lean sources, manifest, commands, toolchain and exact commit reviewed.

Report the strongest *completed* milestone. The oscillator approximation, the mapping of physical outcomes to Fock labels, and the correspondence between the 2022 and 2024 state constructions each require source and physics arguments. A certificate of sequence nonconvergence cannot establish which measurement law a detector implements.
